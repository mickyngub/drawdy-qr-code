# QR Code

Make a scannable QR code for a link, a Wi-Fi login, a contact card and more, with the Drawdy logo or your own picture in the middle, and place it on your Drawdy board.

![Typing drawdy.io into the QR Code panel, adding the Drawdy logo, the Navy dots preset and a caption, then placing the code on the board next to a sticky note.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-qr-code/main/docs/media/qr-code.gif)

## What it's for

- **Events and workshops**: put the Wi-Fi login or the board link on a slide, and people join by scanning.
- **Posters and printed boards**: send people from paper to a website, a form or a survey.
- **Contact cards** that save straight into a phone's contacts.
- **Branded codes**: your logo in the middle and your colours, and still easy to scan.

## How to use

1. Click the **QR Code** button on the right of the board.
2. Pick what the code holds: Link, Text, Wi-Fi, Email, Phone, SMS, Contact or Location, then fill in the fields. The preview updates as you type.
3. Under **Look**, choose a **picture in the middle** (None, the Drawdy logo, or upload your own) and a **preset** (Classic, Drawdy, Navy dots, Mint, Cream or Violet).
4. Fine-tune it under **Options** if you like.
5. Click **Place on board**. The code lands in view, next to anything already there rather than on top of it.
6. To change it later, select the code, click **Edit selected**, make your changes and click **Update on board**. **Place as new** makes a copy instead.

## Options

- **Error correction**: L, M, Q or H. Higher levels survive more damage but make a denser code. With a picture in the middle it stays at H, so the code still scans.
- **Quiet zone**: the blank margin around the code, 2 to 4 modules.
- **Modules**: Square, Dots or Rounded.
- **Size**, **code colour** and **background**, with a contrast check that warns you before phones would struggle.
- **Caption**: optional text under the code.
- **Place as**: shapes you can recolour on the board, or one crisp PNG image.

## Tips

- Keep the code darker than its background; the panel warns you when it isn't.
- Scan it with your phone before you print it.
- A link without `https://` gets it added for you.
- For your own picture, upload a PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or SVG, or drop it on the preview. Square pictures with a transparent background look best.
- A very long code can need more than 3,000 shapes. Then pick Square, shorten the content or place it as an image.

## Privacy

- Nothing loads from the network. What you type stays in the panel and on your board.
- Your options and uploaded picture are saved in this browser for next time.
- What a code holds, such as a Wi-Fi password, can be read by anyone who can see the board.
